# Demonstration policy: chargebacks

This is a fictional classroom policy for the payments desk.

A chargeback is a payment dispute raised through the customer's card issuer. A merchant refund is a separate process initiated by the merchant. Check whether a chargeback is already open before proposing a merchant refund.

When a customer mentions an existing chargeback, collect the dispute reference and escalate to the payments review team. Avoid issuing a duplicate merchant refund while the dispute is under review.

The payments review team owns chargeback responses. This policy does not specify card-network dispute deadlines or chargeback fees.
