# Demonstration policy: missing items

This is a fictional classroom policy for the payments desk.

For a missing item, collect the order ID, the item name, and whether the package itself arrived. Check the packing record before proposing a resolution.

Open a missing-item investigation within 24 hours of receiving the report. The support team may offer a replacement or a refund after reviewing the packing record. This policy does not specify a fixed refund percentage for missing items.

Do not apply the damaged-item 80 percent rule to a missing item. A human reviewer chooses the resolution after the investigation.
