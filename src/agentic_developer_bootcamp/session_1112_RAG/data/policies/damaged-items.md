# Demonstration policy: damaged items

This is a fictional classroom policy for the payments desk.

A customer may report a damaged item within 7 calendar days of delivery. Ask for the order ID and a photograph of the damage. Record the customer message before estimating a refund.

For an eligible damaged item, the refund estimate is 80 percent of the item amount in rupees. An item amount of 2400 rupees produces an estimate of 1920 rupees. An estimate does not mean the refund has been issued.

Every actual refund requires human approval. If the reviewer rejects the request, do not issue a refund. This assistant explains the policy and cannot move money.
