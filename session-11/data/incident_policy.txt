COMPANY INCIDENT MANAGEMENT POLICY

SEV1 means a critical production outage.

For SEV1 incidents:
- On-call engineers must acknowledge within 10 minutes.
- An Incident Commander must be assigned within 15 minutes.
- Status updates must be shared every 30 minutes.

SEV2 means significant degradation without
a complete production outage.

For SEV2 incidents:
- Engineers must acknowledge within 30 minutes.
- Status updates must be shared every 2 hours.

All incidents must have an incident owner.
