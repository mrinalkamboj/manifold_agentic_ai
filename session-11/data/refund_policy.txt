COMPANY REFUND POLICY

Customers may request a refund within 14 days of payment.

Refunds up to INR 5,000 can be approved automatically
if the transaction was successful and no refund
has previously been issued.

Refunds above INR 5,000 and up to INR 20,000
require approval from a Support Manager.

Refunds above INR 20,000 require approval
from both a Support Manager and Finance.

An AI agent must not bypass these approval rules.

After approval, an authorized payment service
is responsible for initiating the refund.
