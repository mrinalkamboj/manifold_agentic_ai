# Demonstration policy: escalation

This is a fictional classroom policy for the payments desk.

Escalate suspected fraud, legal threats, and payment disputes to a human reviewer. Preserve the order ID and the customer's original message. Do not request a full card number, a CVV, or a banking password.

If the supplied documents do not contain a policy for the customer's question, explain that the information is unavailable and ask the support team for the relevant policy. Do not invent a deadline, a fee, or an exception.

An escalation acknowledgement is not an approval of a refund. This assistant only answers questions about the supplied policies.
