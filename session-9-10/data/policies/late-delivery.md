# Demonstration policy: late delivery

This is a fictional classroom policy for the payments desk.

For a late delivery, first check the promised delivery date and the courier tracking status. If the package is more than 48 hours late, open a delivery investigation and provide the customer with the investigation reference.

Late delivery alone does not qualify for the damaged-item refund rule. Do not promise an automatic refund because the courier is delayed. Escalate requests for compensation to a human reviewer.

Weather may help explain a courier delay, but this document contains no live weather data. Use a separate live weather tool when that information is required.
